# 🛒 Flipkart Sales Analysis Dashboard

A comprehensive **Power BI Dashboard** designed to analyze Flipkart product data and uncover insights into pricing trends, customer ratings, discounts, stock availability, and product category performance. This project transforms raw e-commerce data into meaningful business intelligence through interactive visualizations and KPI-driven analysis.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Flipkart Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a detailed view of product performance on Flipkart, helping identify customer preferences, pricing strategies, and market trends.

The dashboard focuses on:

* Product category performance
* Pricing and discount analysis
* Customer rating trends
* Stock availability insights
* High-performing products
* Category-wise sales opportunities

Through interactive visualizations and filters, stakeholders can explore data efficiently and make informed business decisions.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Electronics and Fashion categories contain the highest number of products.
* Products with moderate discounts often receive better ratings than heavily discounted products.
* Customer ratings are concentrated between 4.0 and 5.0.
* Certain categories maintain high ratings despite premium pricing.
* Stock availability significantly varies across categories.
* Discount strategies differ greatly among product segments.
